Interaction contract

  • Nothing runs when the sheet opens or a preset is selected.
  • Conversation expands progressively after Send.
  • Cloud disclosure changes before submission, never after.
  • Only one request may be in flight; double-submit is coalesced.
  • Cancel suppresses late results and cancellation noise.
  • Failed sends restore the outgoing prompt only when the user has not typed something new.
